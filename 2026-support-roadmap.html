Executive Summary

Where the Support Org is Heading in 2026

The Rithum support organization enters 2026 mid-transformation. Following the merger of two distinct business units, the team is executing a structured, year-long program to consolidate people, processes, and platforms into a single, scalable operating model — while simultaneously deploying AI capabilities that will define how we deliver support at scale going forward.

Three strategic themes anchor the year: Unification (one team, one tech stack, one delivery model), AI Enablement (intelligent case deflection, internal knowledge agents, and self-service tooling), and Team Structure (the org design, metrics, and role clarity that make everything else sustainable). By Q4, the org will be operating fully on Salesforce, ServiceNow will be retired, and the L0–L3 support tiering model will be live across both legacy platforms — setting a strong foundation for 2027.

Q1 Complete
6 of 8 Q1 initiatives are fully done — incident management unified, Brands status page migrated, SSE and Manager roles launched, senior manager layer consolidated.
Q2 In Flight
Salesforce consolidation and AI governance are the primary in-flight work streams. SF migration remains the gating dependency for Q3 tech work.
Notable Change
Premium Onboarding & Support has moved to 2027. Design is actively in progress; full launch will follow SF consolidation and a 2026 pilot.
Strategic Themes

Three Pillars Driving 2026

Every initiative this year maps to at least one of these core themes.

🔗

Unification

Consolidating the two legacy support organizations into a single operating unit — unified roles, processes, tooling, and delivery models. The centerpiece is migrating all support activity onto Salesforce and retiring ServiceNow, eliminating the dual-system overhead that currently burdens both agents and customers.

Platform · Process · Org
🤖

AI Enablement

Deploying AI at every layer of the support stack. External-facing bots handle case deflection and first-contact resolution at L0, while internal knowledge agents give platform experts instant access to resolution guidance — reducing handle time and improving consistency of answers.

Case Deflection · Knowledge
🏗️

Team Structure

Building the organizational scaffolding that makes unification stick — standardized metrics, aligned management layers, and a consistent delivery model across both business units. This includes manager consolidation, skills assessments, and the launch of new senior support engineering roles.

AI Language Support 2027 Not Started Extend multilingual support coverage — live translations in Salesforce Knowledge, chat, phone, and email for non-English-speaking clients.
Close Email-to-Case 2027 Not Started Transition inbound email channels to structured Salesforce case creation, eliminating untracked email-based resolution paths.
Premium Support 2027 In Progress Three-tier model (Standard / Premium / Enterprise) with ACV-based pricing. Design in progress; full launch moved to 2027. Conservative 3-year revenue projection: $2.87M–$11.44M depending on attach rate.
Phone Support Migration 2027 Not Started Moving inbound phone support from Amazon Connect (~$1.2K/mont sh) to Salesforce Agentforce Contact Center to create a unified case record from first contact. Deprecate phone support for suppliers and move to paid model (Premium Support)
