The opportunity

Why this matters

Today, Rithum's onboarding is largely self-serve and one-to-many — documentation-led, not relationship-led. For mid-market and enterprise clients with complex integrations, multi-channel catalogs, and diverse user populations, that creates friction at the most critical moment in the client lifecycle: the first 90 days.

How it works

The program structure

Standard and Premium onboarding are a white-glove project-management experience. The goal: a client who is operational and confident from day one. Three things define the offering.

1

Dedicated Onboarding Consultant

A single point of contact who owns the entire onboarding timeline — sequencing the work, coordinating across Rithum teams, and surfacing blockers before they become delays. This is the clearest differentiator from the self-serve experience, and it mirrors best-in-class enterprise SaaS (e.g., Salesforce Signature, AWS/Google Cloud TAMs).

2

White Glove Product Content

A deep dive into the client's current tech stack — catalog structure, data feeds, and integration mapping — to ensure data hygiene from day one. This goes beyond standard configuration and reduces the rework that commonly shows up in post-go-live support.

3

Tailored Training

Where useful, the consultant coordinates training calibrated to the client's actual user personas — Admins, Merchandising Leads, End Users — rather than generic platform walkthroughs. It's part of the broader engagement, not a separately-priced add-on.

Opt-in from launch. No accounts are auto-enrolled or grandfathered. Standard is required for certain channels; Standard and Premium are otherwise voluntary add-ons.

The tiers

Three tiers, escalating depth

Tier selection is based on account size, integration complexity, and the level of hands-on support needed during implementation. Onboarding is priced per supplier, per engagement — a flat fee per onboarding event, not a recurring subscription.

Premium $3,500 / supplier None at launch* Everything in Standard, plus White Glove Product Content uploads.

* Premium launches as a one-time fee only — no ongoing monthly subscription. We can re-evaluate adding an ongoing support option based on client demand after launch.

Retailer bulk seats. Retailers may purchase onboarding seats at any tier and assign them to specific suppliers during the sales process, subject to the following conditions:
  • Seats are use-it-or-lose-it — time-bound, with no indefinite rollover.
  • Seats are restricted to the purchasing retailer's onboarding queue and are not transferable to other retailers.
  • Seats cannot be re-assigned once onboarding has started for a brand.
  • Support subscriptions must be purchased by the account receiving service.

Guardrails

Legal considerations for contracts

The following guardrails need to be reflected in contract language before we scale. Exact language to be drafted with Legal.

1

SKU limits

The standard Premium fee covers up to 100,000 SKUs per upload. Engagements above this threshold require separate scoping and pricing.

2

SKU density / run rate

Contracts must define SKU density and run-rate thresholds over a period of time, so service expectations remain manageable for high-volume suppliers.

3

Liability language

Contracts must include language allocating data responsibility: Rithum does the lift and uploads the data, but ultimate responsibility for data accuracy remains with the brand.
